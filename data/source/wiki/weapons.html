Weapons

Last updated: Exalt Version 7.0.0.0 (Sep 2026)

Weapons are equipment items that are the primary means of damaging monsters. There are twelve weapon types that are shared between the different character classes, including weapon subtypes. For each type, there are currently 15 tiers of weapons (T0 - T14), and for each subtype, there are currently 8 tiers of weapons (T7 - T14). There are also numerous untiered and set tiered items.

For a list of attributes given to certain weapons/abilities go to Weapon Attributes.
