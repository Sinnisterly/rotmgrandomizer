Dungeons

Last updated: Exalt Version 6.12.0.0 (Jul 2026)

Dungeons consist of a network of rooms (in some cases a single room) containing various monsters based on the theme of the dungeon. The last room will contain one or more boss enemies, which may drop special items. Many dungeons also have one or more “treasure rooms” containing minibosses that drop unique loot. Dungeon loot varies widely in quality, but harder dungeons generally drop better loot.

To reach a dungeon without using a key, it is necessary to kill certain enemies, which have a certain chance of dropping an entrance to that dungeon. For certain enemies, this chance is guaranteed. Any player can enter the dungeon by clicking “Enter” or using the interact key when standing on an entrance portal.

Dungeons may also appear with Modifiers that alter certain gameplay aspects of the dungeon. More info on the modifiers and their chances of appearing can be found on the Dungeon Modifiers page.

All portals stay open for 30 seconds before vanishing, except for Crystal Cavern and areas of Oryx’s Castle. The Locked Wine Cellar Portal will remain for two minutes if not opened. An unlocked Wine Cellar portal and the portal to Oryx’s Sanctuary remain open indefinitely.

Most dungeons have a player limit of 50 players. Attempting to enter a full dungeon will only result in a “Dungeon is full” message appearing in the chat. The exceptions to this limit are:

  • Alien Wormhole dungeons, Court of Oryx dungeons, The Tavern, and Spectral Penitentiary, which have a limit of 25 players.
  • White Snake Invasion III, which has a limit of 20 players.
  • Stromwell’s Rift I, Stromwell’s Rift II, Stromwell’s Rift III which have a limit of 85 players.
  • Heroic Dungeons and Hidden Interregnum, which have a limit of 15 players.
  • All Oryx’s Castle areas, which have a limit equal to the Realm player limit (85 players).
  • Santa’s Workshop, which is only accessible in the player’s Vault and by definition, has a limit of 1 player.
  • The Admin Arena, which can only be opened by players with admin powers and has a limit of 100 players.

All dungeons have an associated difficulty rating, indicated by the number of gravestones on the loading screen for the dungeon. This rating ranges from 0 (harmless) to 10 (very difficult). Note that a non-seasonal dungeon has difficulty 7 or higher if and only if it is an Exaltation dungeon.

Some dungeons have a multi-stage layout with portals connecting different stages. Details of these are described on the individual dungeon pages.

Dealing soulbound damage to the boss results in a completion statistic being counted on that player’s character. The Dungeon Collection fame bonuses are awarded to characters that have completed certain dungeons.

Dungeons can easily be called out in the chat by clicking on the portal in the world, producing a unique message with an image of the portal, the dungeon’s name, and its grade (but not the specific modifiers, which may be worth calling in the chat manually). Such messages cannot be produced in any other way (so there is no possibility of trolling). This makes it easy for other players to teleport to the caller and enter the dungeon together. However, if you call a dungeon, you should wait to enter, as other players in the realm cannot teleport to you if you have already gone in.

Advanced Dungeons

Advanced dungeons are types of dungeons that are much more difficult, but more rewarding versions of already existing dungeons. They come with various benefits/rules which include the following:

  • 50% reduction to all healing.
  • 30% loot boost and 90% exp boost.
  • Increased drop rate of tiered items.
  • 5x drop rate for Artifacts.
  • Minimum Uncommon rarity loot.

Wormholes

Wormholes are portals to four alien worlds that serve as a “time trial” where players must defeat multiple waves of increasingly powerful enemies within a time limit to earn rewards. The time limit decreases with the more players present in the dungeon. All of the dungeons listed below drop at a guaranteed rate.

Once the Alien UFO is defeated, wormholes subsequently appear across the realm, and are visible on the minimap as colored dots. Each wormhole is guarded by a small party of enemies from the dungeon.

Malogia 5
Untaris 5
Katalund 5
Forax 5

Advanced Wormholes

Neo Wormholes follow a similar structure to the original versions but with more health, more damage, remixed patterns, additional enemies, limited healing and enchanced rewards. They can grant exaltations if you beat the timer and kill the Satellite Core at the very end. Standard advanced benefits:

  • 30% increased drop rates
  • 90% increased XP
  • Minimum of one slot for all gear
  • Greatly increased artifact rates

Heroic Dungeons

These dungeons consist solely of a more difficult version of their non-heroic counterparts’ boss encounters, although they do provide better rewards as compensation. All heroic dungeons disable pets upon entering.

Both heroic dungeons used to have a chance of dropping from the bosses of the respective non-heroic dungeon (Septavius the Ghost God and Archdemon Malphas), but they currently do not drop, meaning they are only accessible from keys. However, the keys are not currently sold in the Nexus, but they do drop from Key Fairies. These dungeons are typically only accessible during special events.

History

  • Dungeons were added to the game in Build 74 (Feb 2010), representing the largest addition of content to the game since the extra Classes were first introduced.
  • When the first dungeons were added, monsters dropped Dungeon Keys, which were then used to open the portal. This was changed in Build 110 (Dec 2010), with the exception of the Beach Bum and the Key Fairy.
  • Since Build 111 (Feb 2011), Keys are available in the Nexus for Realm Gold.
  • Prior to Patch X.32.7.0 (Feb 2020), most dungeons had a loosely enforced player limit of 85 players. It was easy to bypass this limit if many players entered a portal at once, because the player counter took several seconds to update.
