Quivers

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.3.0.0 (Nov 2024)

Quivers are used by Archers to fire a powerful, piercing, single-shot arrow that also cripple enemy movement. Tiered quivers inflict Paralyzed to their enemies and have a duration of 3 seconds. Most quivers also grant a dexterity bonus to the wearer.

The untiered quivers have various different effects, see details in the table below for more information.
