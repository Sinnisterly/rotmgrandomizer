Tomes

Last updated: Exalt Version 6.1.0.0 (Sep 2025)

Tomes are used by Priests to instantly restore health to themselves, apply Healing to themselves and nearby players, and deal damage. Wisdom modifier (WisMod) applies to Tomes and this increases the Range, the Heal amount, the Healing duration and strength, and damage of the Damage Nova.

In Exalt Version 6.1.0.0 (Sep 2025), all tomes were reworked. Heal now has two effects: an instant self-heal and Healing applied to self and group (separate value; does not stack - only the strongest effect applies). Tomes now deal damage with on-cursor nova (Damage Nova: explodes 2 times for 0.8 seconds within 2.5 sqrs). Heal / Healing / Range / Damage can be boosted via stat mods.
