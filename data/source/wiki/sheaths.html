Sheaths

This page is currently a work in progress.

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.11.0.0 (May 2025)

Sheaths are ability items used by Kensei. When used, Kensei will charge up a certain number of dashes. If used with charges left, Kensei will instead dash in the direction of cursor up to a maximum distance which can be different for each sheath. Kensei is immune to enemy shots during his dash, but stays susceptible to environmental damage (like lava or killer honey) and area of effect attacks (such as Medusa bombs). Dashing itself is independent of cooldown and doesn’t use any mana.

When Kensei hits an enemy during his dash, he gains a stack. Each stack multiplies a certain stat of his. For tiered sheaths, this is always ATT Boost but every other stat except MP has a sheath boosting it. Every enemy hit by dash also receives a flat amount of damage. Each stack tracks its duration independently of any other obtained stacks, and once a stack hits its maximum duration it will decay without affecting the other stacks, if any. Dashing into an enemy while already at the maximum number of stacks has no effect on the remaining duration of any of the existing stacks.

All sheaths have a cooldown of 3 seconds, unless stated otherwise.
