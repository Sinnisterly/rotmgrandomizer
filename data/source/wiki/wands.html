Wands

Last updated: Exalt Version 6.1.0.0 (Sep 2025)

Wands are used by Priests, Sorcerers, Summoners and Druids. While having the longest range compared to other weapons, they have lower damage output compared to them. All tiered wands fire a single shot, have a projectile speed of 18 tiles per second, a range of 9 tiles, and Shots hit multiple targets

Click on the picture/name for additional information.
