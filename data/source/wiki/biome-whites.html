Biome Whites

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.5.0.0 (Dec 2024)

Biome Whites are cosmically rare items that drop in specific biomes in The Realm, rivaling event whites in terms of power and rarity. Most enemies in each biome have an incredibly small chance of dropping their respective biome white, allowing for players to focus on specific biomes for any desired items.

The stronger the enemy, the higher the chance of it dropping a Biome White. Heroes of Oryx and Beacon Guardians have the highest drop rate, followed by stronger and rarer enemies (Such as Revenants, Sting Spiders, or Abyssal Krakens), and finally by normal enemies.
