Summoner

Last updated: Exalt Version 6.1.0.0 (Sep 2025)

Summoner is one of the 19 player classes.

The Summoner’s mace can create minions that the user can control the movement of with the summon control key, making the users minions follow their cursor. These minions will deal damage to enemies within their range.

Minions summoned will have a Summon Cost that can be increased with enchantments or gear, allowing for more minions to be spawned.

See Summoner Class Guide for a walk-through on how to use the Summoner effectively.
