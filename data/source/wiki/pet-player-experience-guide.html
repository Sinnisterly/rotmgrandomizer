Pet Player Experience, what’s it all about?

PPE (“Pet Player Experience”, although NeutralMan initially called it “Pro Player Experience) is a self-imposed challenge for experienced players. The bare rules are:

  • Start on a Lv.1 character
  • No material help from other characters:
    • No trading.
    • Other than cosmetics and your pet, no items from your other characters.

Some people add additional self-imposed rules including:

  • No guild/Discord dungeons
  • No UT Forge
    • Only forge items when you already obtained the blueprint (on the PPE)
  • No leeching
  • Group PPE (a set group that can share items within)

Or the following variations of a PPE:

  • NPE: Non-pet Player Experience (no using pets)
  • UPE: Untiered Player Experience (only equip UT’s after starting)
  • TPE: Tiered Player Experience (only equip tiered items, and in order of tiers)
