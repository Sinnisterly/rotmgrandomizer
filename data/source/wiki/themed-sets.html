Themed Sets

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.12.0.0 (June 2025)

Some enemies drop equipment items which are themed with similar aesthetics and can be worn as a complete themed set.

Unlike set tier items (ST sets), these themed sets do not have any special functions beyond the individual equipment items. They do not transform the player to a larger sprite, nor do they provide additional stat bonuses. Like other untiered items, these cannot be purchased for Realm Gold.

The first three themed sets were introduced during the Wild Shadow era (up to the Purified Priest), three were released under Kabam (Ghostly Trickster, Mad Scientist and the limited Summer Solstice Wizard), and Deca has continued the concept with sets themed to the Lost Halls, and, more recently, with limited availability reskins during seasonal events.

The sets are organised in chronological order of release, with limited availability items at the end of the list.
