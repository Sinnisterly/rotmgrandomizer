Skulls

Last updated: Exalt Version 6.8.0.0 (Mar 2026)

Skulls give Necromancers the ability to raise an autonomous minion that attacks enemies for you and heals you. This minion is spawned at the cursor’s location, and these units automatically target and attack the nearest enemies, with a percentage of their damage being lifestealed to restore the player’s health (except for Memento Mori). In tiered skulls and some untiered ones, this health restoration from undead minions contributes to filling a secondary meter that enables the use of Necromancer’s Vampire Blast, providing a quick burst of healing for both the player and their party.

Tiered skull damage scale with WIS mod: every point of WIS over 55 will give the minion +3 damage on shot and +6 damage on Vampire Blast. In addition, the Heal Range of the Vampire Blast also scales with WIS, gaining +0.1 tiles of range every point of WIS over 50.

Before Exalt Version 6.5.0.0 (Jan 2026), skulls gave Necromancers the ability to instantly deal AoE damage and instantly heal off enemies for himself and nearby allies. This rework gave this class a great damage buff per mana spent on abilities, but in general, it nerfed the heals. But since the heals now are overtime, it is more sustainable than before.
