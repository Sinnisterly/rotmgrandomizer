Robes

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.13.0.0 (June 2025)

Robes are used by Wizards, Priests, Necromancers, Mystics, Sorcerers, Bards and Summoners to provide a good amount of defense, magic, and wisdom. Higher-tier Robes also provide some attack.

Click on the picture/name for additional information.
