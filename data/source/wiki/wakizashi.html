Wakizashi

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.4.0.0 (Nov 2024)

Wakizashi are ability items used by Samurai to perform a slash attack across the player’s line of aim. The intersection of this attack is centered on the crosshair unless the crosshair is too far from the player, in which case they intersect at maximum range. Higher tiered (and most untiered) wakizashi can inflict the Exposed status effect, which reduces the target’s defense by 20.

Unless stated otherwise, all wakizashi:
- have a maximum cast range of 4.4 tiles
- have an offset angle of 90° (shots sweep left to right below the cursor)
- shots have a gap angle of 45°
- shots are spread 0.4 tiles apart along the gap angle
- Shots hit multiple targets and Shots pass through obstacles
- projectiles have a range of 6.3 tiles

The untiered wakizashi have various different effects. See details in the table below for more information.

In Exalt Version 5.12.0.0 (June 2025) all Wakizashis received Projectile Maximum Spawn Distance of 6 tiles.
