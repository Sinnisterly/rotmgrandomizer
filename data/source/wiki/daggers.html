Daggers

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.11.0.0 (May 2025)

Daggers are used by Rogues, Assassins, and Tricksters. They typically have a high variance in damage and a longer range than swords and katanas. All tiered daggers fire a single shot, have a projectile speed of 14 tiles per second, and a range of 5.6 tiles. Other daggers are the same unless explicitly stated otherwise.

After Exalt Version 5.0.0.0 (July 2024) all tiered daggers have Rate of Fire 105%.

Click on the picture/name for additional information.
