Bows

Last updated: Exalt Version 6.4.0.0 (Dec 2025)

Bows are used by Archers, Huntresses and Bards. They are multi-shot weapons with a good range at the cost of not having a particularly large damage output.

All tiered bows have a shot count of 3, a projectile speed of 16 tiles per second, all Shots hit multiple targets while the side shots Ignores defense of target.
Tiered bows have a max range of 7.04 tiles. They possess an arc gap of 7°, resulting in a true range at 4.07 tiles.
All bows are the same unless stated otherwise.

Click on the picture/name for additional information.
