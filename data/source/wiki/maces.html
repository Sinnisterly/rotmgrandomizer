Maces

Last updated: Exalt Version 6.11.0.0 (Jun 2026)

Maces are ability items used by Summoners to summon monsters to fight for her. She is able to command these monsters by pressing the Command Summon button. By default, this function is set to the Middle Mouse button, which can be executed by pressing down your Mouse’s wheel.

Depending on the exact Mace you are using, the functionality may differ. Minions spawned by a Tiered Mace will travel to the cursor location upon pressing the Middle Mouse button, while some Untiered and Limited Maces may instead have the minions follow a path uncontrollable by the user, such as Orbiting the player, Orbiting the enemy, or being stationary once placed.

Unless otherwise mentioned, all Mace shots carry a range of 4.05, Shots hit multiple targets and Shots pass through obstacles.

In Exalt Version 6.11.0.0 (Jun 2026), maces received a StatMod and were reworked. A new sub-stat, “Summon Power” (SP), has been added to the game. Summons now have an associated Summon Cost. Players can continue to create summons until they reach their Summon Cap. All classes have a default Summon Cap of 9. For now, the only ways to increase Summon Power are through the new Phytomancer ST Set and a new Summon Power StatMod multiplier tradeoff enchantment. This enchantment slightly reduces the individual power of summons in exchange for a larger army.
