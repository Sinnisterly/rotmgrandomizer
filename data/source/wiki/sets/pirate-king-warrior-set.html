Notes
The Pirate King Warrior Set is the nineteenth ST set to be released.

In addition to the stat bonuses this set provides, the weapon has extra range at the cost of shots being wavy and at 80% rate of fire, and the tricorne fires cannonballs upon taking 40 damage in one hit, every 3 seconds at most.

A warrior with WC tops and a Ring of the Pyramid would have 890 HP, 272 MP, 79 ATT, 63 DEF, 50 SPD, 50 DEX, 75 VIT, 50 WIS, for a DPS of 3003-2653 (0-60 DEF). This set has a superior DPS of 3118-2950; coming from a powerful but very slow shot, it is relatively better against high DEF. The Tricorne’s reactive proc on-hit boosts damage further, while increased MP helps with using the ability. However, this set is much slower than usual.

Overall, this set is a sidegrade (slower, more powerful) to WC tops, but is inferior to Void tops and better items.

History
On Testing, the set’s skin was that of Jon Bilgewater and it had different stat bonuses:
2nd Piece Bonus: +70 HP, +50 MP
3rd Piece Bonus: +100 HP, +80 MP
4th Piece Bonus: +100 ATT, -30 DEX, -20 SPD
Subtotal of Bonuses: +170 HP, +130 MP, +100 ATT, -30 DEX, -20 SPD
Overall Stat Bonus: +180 HP, +130 MP, +101 ATT, +26 DEF, -25 DEX, -17 SPD, +6 VIT

Before Exalt Version 6.2.0.0 (Oct 2025), this set had bonuses:
2 Piece Bonus: +70 HP, +50 MP
3 Piece Bonus: +100 HP, +80 MP
4 Piece Bonus: +100 ATT, -30 DEX, -20 SPD
