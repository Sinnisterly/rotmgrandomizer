Shields

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.9.0.0 (Mar 2025)

Shields provide extra defense for Knights and fire shots in an arc. Most shields inflict the Stunned status against enemies. Higher-tier shields have a wider projectile arc with multiple shots.

Unless stated otherwise, all shields have Effect Duration: 3 seconds, Projectile Speed: 16, Range: 3.2, Shots pass through obstacles, and Shots hit multiple targets.

After Exalt Version 5.9.0.0 (Mar 2025), all tiered shields give the user 25% (Kogbold Cower Shield - 30%) damage reduction for 5 seconds. This damage reduction is calculated after def is applied. Most shields received a statMod that increases base damage.
