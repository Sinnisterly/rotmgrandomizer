Classes

Realm of the Mad God has a large variety of classes, each with a unique special ability. There are 19 of them to pick and choose. Each class has its own advantages and disadvantages.

Unlocking Classes

On your first steps into Realm, you will begin on the Wizard class. After reaching a certain level on each class, you will unlock other classes. For the classes on the first row, the level you must achieve on its precursor (class required to unlock next class) class is 5; new accounts on Steam will have these classes unlocked by default. The classes on the bottom two rows require you to reach level 20 on their precursor classes.

Level 5 required for these:

  • Priest ← Wizard
  • Archer ← Priest
  • Rogue ← Archer
  • Warrior ← Rogue

Level 20 required for these:

  • Knight ← Warrior
  • Paladin ← Priest and Knight
  • Assassin ← Rogue and Wizard
  • Necromancer ← Wizard and Priest
  • Huntress ← Rogue and Archer
  • Mystic ← Necromancer and Huntress
  • Trickster ← Paladin and Assassin
  • Sorcerer ← Assassin and Necromancer
  • Ninja ← Rogue and Warrior
  • Samurai ← Knight and Ninja
  • Bard ← Paladin and Huntress
  • Summoner ← Sorcerer and Mystic
  • Kensei ← Trickster and Ninja
  • Druid ← Sorcerer and Mystic
