Heavy Armors

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.10.0.0 (Apr 2025)

Heavy Armor is used by Warriors, Knights, Paladins, Samurai, and Kensei. This type of armor provides a high amount of defense, but tiered armor lacks any additional bonuses.
