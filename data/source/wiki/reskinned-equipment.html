Reskinned Equipment

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.10.0.0 (Apr 2025)

Reskinned Equipment are functionally identical to the original versions and usually differ only by sprite/projectile. Some reskins may differ from the originals in xp bonus, feed power, or soulbound status, but retain the original stats and effects. Some reskins can also gain special enchantments using artifacts to become more powerful and unique.

Reskins are typically released for holidays and other special events, such as Month of the Mad God.
