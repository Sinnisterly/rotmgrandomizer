Seals

Last updated: Exalt Version 6.1.0.0 (Sep 2025)

Seals are used by Paladins to grant a dramatic increase to the Healing rate (20 HP/sec), Damaging (+25%), and an HP Boost to the user and surrounding players for a short period of time. The HP increase is not an instant heal. Wisdom modifier (WISmod) applies to Seals and this increases the Range, the Duration, and the HP Boost.
Triggers at at least 50 WIS:
HP Boost:

  • 1 WIS = +2 max HP
  • 10 WIS = +1 range
  • 10 WIS = +1 sec duration

Healing / Damaging:

  • 1 WIS = +0.1 sec duration
  • 1 WIS = +0.1 range

Unless otherwise stated, tiered seals all do Healing party effect, Damaging party effect and HP Boost party effect with Range 4.5 (7 / 7.5 @75 WIS).

History

Before Release 25.0.0 (Oct 2014), Seals had just healing and damaging effect and had a longer effect time: up to 10 seconds (Tier 5/6). This allowed players to keep themselves buffed forever with a level 60 MP Heal pet.

After Release 25.0.0 (Oct 2014), Tiered seals gave the status effects: Damaging and Healing. In addition, there was also a HP boost with a Wisdom Modifier attached.
