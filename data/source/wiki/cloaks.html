Cloaks

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.1.0.0 (Sep 2024)

Cloaks are worn by Rogues, which give them the ability to turn themselves Invisible to be undetected by enemies.

After Exalt Version 5.0.0.0 (July 2024) cloaks will keep their basic functionality that sets themselves Invisible and makes them undetectable by enemies, but now after shooting, after some time, player will exit Invisibility. When exiting on tiered cloaks player will get two effects. The first one ( Lethal Strike) adds a flat amount of damage to projectiles created by player while the effective is active, additionally a portion of the target enemies defense is converted to bonus damage. While Lethal Strike is active, the player will fire additional projectiles towards the cursor location. Note that one can switch cloaks while invisible to use the initial effects of one along with the ending effects of the other. For example, one may teleport with the Cloak of the Planewalker, then switch to a tiered cloak to gain Lethal Strike.
