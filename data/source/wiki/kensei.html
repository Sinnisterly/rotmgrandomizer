Kensei

Last updated: Exalt Version 6.1.0.0 (Sep 2025)

Kensei is one of the 19 player classes.

The Kensei’s sheath gives the user a set amount of charges. These charges allow the user to dash over enemies dealing damage via a trail and through projectiles created at the end of the dash.

Dashing prevents damage from projectiles, but not enviromental hazards like lava, or AOEs.

Dashing successfully over an enemy will give the player an ATT Boost multiplier. With this multiplier increasing for all enemies dashed over with a max of five.

See Kensei Class Guide for a walk-through on how to use the Kensei effectively.
