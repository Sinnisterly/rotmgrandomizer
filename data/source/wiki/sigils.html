Sigils

This page is currently a work in progress.

Last updated: Exalt Version 6.7.0.0 (Feb 2026)

Sigils are ability items used by Druids. Sigils allow the druid to transform after they have built up enough meter through casting ability or hitting enemies with weapon. When transformed the druid will gain improved attacks, stat bonuses, and other effects at the cost of some of your weapons range. The druid has a wide range of transformations available through it’s UTs that let you build very different playstyles and class dynamics.
