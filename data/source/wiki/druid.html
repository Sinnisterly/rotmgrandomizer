Druid

This page is currently a work in progress.

Last updated: Exalt Version 6.7.0.0 (Feb 2026)

Druid is one of the 19 player classes.

The Druid’s sigil allows for a projectile to be shot towards the user’s cursor and the ability to transform into different animal forms through a meter. This meter can be filled through ability use, or hitting enemies.

Upon transforming, the user gains a close-range bite attack, a large ATT Boost and DEF Boost for a short period of time in exchange for -33% weapon range.

The Druid Class Guide has not been created yet.
