Katanas

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.11.0.0 (May 2025)

Katanas are used by Ninja, Samurai, and Kensei. Katanas stand out from Daggers and Swords, possessing a higher damage output than a dagger, but having longer range than a sword. Additionally, they can pierce through targets to clear groups.

All tiered katanas fire a single shot, have a projectile speed of 13 tiles per second, a range of 4.745 tiles, and Shots hit multiple targets. Other katanas are the same unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Click on the picture/name for additional information.
