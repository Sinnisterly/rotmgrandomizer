Traps

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.3.0.0 (Nov 2024)

Traps are ability items used by Huntresses. Using this ability will throw a trap in an arc, targeting the mouse cursor, similar to assassin poisons. After a set time, the trap lands and starts pulsing to indicate that it is set; this lasts 10 seconds for tiered traps. It is triggered when a monster walks into the set trap and will explode, damaging and debuffing every enemy caught in the explosion. Every tiered trap inflicts Slowed, and all traps have a trigger range half of their explosion radius. If a trap is thrown directly at an enemy, it will explode immediately upon landing. Set traps that have not been triggered by enemies will explode at the end of their duration.

All traps have a throw time of 1 second and a duration of 10 seconds unless stated otherwise.
