Lutes

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.2.1.0 (Oct 2024)

Lutes are ability items used by the Bard class. When used, the Lute provides the Inspired status effect to allies. This effect increases players’ attack range by 1.25x the normal range of their weapon. The duration of Inspired increases with tier. Higher-tiered Lutes will also provide a DEF Boost when used, along with a passive DEF boost on equip. All tiered Lutes have no cooldown.

All lutes have a Inspired radius of 4.5 tiles, with the exception of the UT Snake Charmer Pungi which does not grant Inspired, the ST Angel’s Fanfare which has a radius of 4 tiles and the UT Chief’s War Horn which has a radius of 5 tiles.
