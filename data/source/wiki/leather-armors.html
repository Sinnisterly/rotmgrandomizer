Leather Armors

Last updated: Exalt Version 6.4.0.0 (Dec 2025)

Leather Armors are used by Rogues, Archers, Assassins, Huntresses, Tricksters, Ninjas and Druids. They provide a moderate amount of defense. Higher tiers also give a dexterity bonus.
