April Fool's Equipment Versions

During April Fool’s events, along with resprites and renaming of enemies, some equipment items also receive a temporary change.

This can be a change of name, description, sprite, and even in some cases stats. These are humorous/joking and often self-referential, poking fun at the item, or the game community in some way.

At the end of the April Fools event these changes are reverted back to the standard item, so the April Fools versions do not “exist” in the sense of reskinned equipment that one can obtain and keep in perpetuity.

Equipment that has been “April Fooled”:

Items that have been temporarily replaced by April Fool’s versions:
  • Crystal Sword changed into Mithril Sword
  • Dagger of Foul Malevolence changed into Dagger of the Malevolent Fool
  • Hydra Skin Armor changed into BUFFED Hydra Skin Armor
  • Leaf Bow changed into Leave Bow
  • Ring of Decades changed into Ring of Overwhelming Health
  • Staff of the Cosmic Whole changed into Staff of the Cosmetic Whole
  • Wand of Recompense changed into Wand of Redundance
  • Bow of Deep Enchantment changed into Bow of Surface Level Enchantment
  • Doom Bow changed into Mood Bow
  • Midnight Star changed into Reasonable Hour Star
  • Bottled Medusozoan changed into Bottled Medusa
  • Ice Crown changed into Nice Crown
  • Tome of the Mushroom Tribes changed into Non-Fungible Tome
  • Lumiaire changed into Luminaire
  • Superior changed into Inferior
  • Loot Drop Potion changed into Potion of Loot Drop Potion
Items that have received a description change for April Fool’s:
  • Dirk
Items that have received a sprite change for April Fool’s:
  • Seal of Blasphemous Prayer resprited as Chocolate Cream Sandwich Cookie and vice versa
  • The untiered Mad Lab items were given their original sprites:
    • Conducting Wand
    • Scepter of Fulmination
    • Robe of the Mad Scientist
