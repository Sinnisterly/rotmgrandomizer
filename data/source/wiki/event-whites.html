Event Whites

This page is currently a work in progress.

Last updated: Exalt Version 7.0.0.0 (Sep 2026)

Event Whites (also referred to as Realm Whites or Encounter Whites) are extraordinarily rare items that drop from specific encounters and minibosses in The Realm. They are comparable to biome whites as they are both incredibly rare and often powerful drops from The Realm.

Some of these items can be obtained as drops from The Keyper, a special encounter that is available during certain events.
