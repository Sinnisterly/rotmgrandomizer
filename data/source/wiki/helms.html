Helms

Last updated: Exalt Version 6.1.0.0 (Sep 2025)

Helms are used by Warriors to apply the Speedy status to themselves, and the Berserk status to themselves and others around them. Each helm also gives a defense bonus to the wearer upon equip. Wisdom modifier (WISmod) applies to Helms and this increases the Range and the Duration.

  • Triggers at 30 WIS and above
  • 1 WIS -> 0.1 range
  • 1 WIS -> 0.1 second duration

All helms with wismod that grant berserk to allies has a range of 4.5 (+0.1 per WIS over 30) tiles

Some untiered helms have a cooldown lasting 1 second longer than its duration time and thus have a downtime of one second. All tiered helms provide Berserk to all allies within range, and Speedy to the user, while untiered helms have some different effects.
