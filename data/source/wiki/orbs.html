Orbs

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.2.0.0 (Oct 2024)

Orbs are used by Mystics to target an area, putting enemies into a status condition called Stasis. In Stasis any affected enemies turn gray and cannot attack or move, but cannot be damaged. Almost every orb also inflicts Curse within a wider radius of the targeted spot. Cursed enemies suffer an increase of 25% to all damage inflicted on them, applied after defense.

  • Triggers at 50 WIS and above
  • 1 WIS -> +0.1 Curse range
  • 10 WIS -> +1 second duration (Curse and self-buffs)

All Orbs have a Stasis Radius: 3, unless otherwise noted. A Mystic’s wisdom stat affects the power of her ability, with Curse Radius and Curse Duration both increasing with WIS.

All tiered Orbs that inflict curse have a Curse Radius of 5 (+0.1 per WIS over 50) tiles.

Stasis duration and range is not affected by this wisdom modifier. Other Orb effects are also modified with WIS (see table for details).

Most bosses are immune to Stasis, though their minions tend to be vulnerable. After a Stasis wears off, the enemy gains Stasis-immunity for a short time, thus chain Stasis is not possible. The Void Entity is the only creature in the game that has an immunity against Curse. Some creatures such as the Crystal Worm Mother can be affected by Stasis and Curse even when they are otherwise invincible.
