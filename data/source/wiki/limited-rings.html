Limited Rings

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.11.0.0 (May 2025)

Limited Rings are items that can only be obtained intermittently from seasonal events or are no longer available. Some limited rings can still be obtainable by trading with other players or as drops from dungeons whose keys are no longer sold.

For a list of untiered/set tiered rings, see Untiered Rings.
