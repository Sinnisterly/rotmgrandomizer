Staves

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.15.0.0 (Aug 2025)

Staves are used by Wizards, Necromancers, and Mystics. They are a long range weapon with good damage owed to firing two shots that move in a sine wave pattern.

All tiered staves fire two shots, have an arc gap of 0 degrees, a projectile speed of 18 tiles per second, a range of 8.55 tiles, an amplitude of 0.5 tiles, and a frequency of 2 cycles. Other staves are the same unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Click on the picture or name for additional information.
