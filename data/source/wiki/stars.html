Stars

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.3.0.0 (Nov 2024)

This page refers to the ability item, not the class quest system.

Stars are ability items used by Ninjas and function as long-range damage abilities with additional effects. For tiered stars, players will grant themselves the Speedy status effect for as long as they hold down the ability hotkey. While the ability key is held down, MP will be used at a rate of 12-19 MP/second for tiered Stars, depending on their tier. Upon release, they shoot a deadly ninja star (if they have enough MP remaining to fire it). During the Speedy status effect, Ninjas do not regenerate MP from their WIS stat, but can recover MP from Magic Potions and pets.

All Stars (excluding Crystalline Kunai and Rage Claws) have the effect of Shots pass through obstacles.
