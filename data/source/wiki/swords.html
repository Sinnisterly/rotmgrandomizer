Swords

Last updated: Exalt Version 5.11.0.0 (May 2025)

Swords are used by Warriors, Knights, and Paladins. They have extremely high damage but are offset with low range.

All tiered swords fire a single shot, have a projectile speed of 10 tiles per second, and a range of 3.5 tiles. Other swords are identical unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Click on the sword or name for additional information. A weapon’s true range (if different from the effective range) is noted in parentheses.
